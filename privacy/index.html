Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you join the early-access list, we collect your name, your gym’s name, your email address and what you teach. We also record that you agreed to hear from us, and when.

Why we collect it

We use it to email you about early access to Kipwell and to set up a time to talk about your gym. We don’t sell or rent your details, and we don’t add you to anyone else’s list.

Where it’s kept

Your details are saved to a private Google Sheet that only Kipwell can open. If we email you, we use an email service to send it.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs each visitor’s IP address for security, as it does for every site it hosts.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which parts of the site they use. It sets cookies and records things like the pages you view, how you found the site, your device and browser, and your approximate location. We don’t send Google your name or email, and we don’t use Analytics for advertising.

To opt out, block cookies for this site in your browser or install Google’s opt-out add-on.

Your choices

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. To see, change or delete your details, email caseywfrancis@gmail.com and we’ll take care of it.

Children

This site is for gym owners and staff. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
